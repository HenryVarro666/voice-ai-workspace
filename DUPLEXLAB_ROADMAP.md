# DuplexLab public roadmap

DuplexLab remains the offline speech-data and ASR-training branch of Local Speaker Lab; native appointment interaction is the separate product showcase. No additional standalone DuplexLab repository is created.

Read the [current methods and results](https://github.com/HenryVarro666/local-speaker-lab/blob/main/experiments/duplexlab/README.md), [decision index](https://github.com/HenryVarro666/local-speaker-lab/blob/main/docs/EXPERIMENT_INDEX.md), and [cross-project roadmap](ROADMAP.md).

Latest four-arm/three-seed results do not establish reliable overall selection or token-weighting benefit. First freeze tokenizer persistence and mixed-length processor behavior, then review train/dev references and acoustic omissions. Previously observed tests stay observed. Private career-planning sections are excluded from this public snapshot.
